Just intonation ear trainer

Use SELECT to show prime limits and choose a pool of pitches, LISTEN to hear the selected pitches, and TEST to identify each interval by clicking its node. Intervals can sound simultaneously or successively, above or below a fixed or variable fundamental. Copyright © Alexander Garsden 2026.

Mode

Test

Not started

Interval Playback

Above or below the fundamental; Both chooses at random for each interval. Successive intervals play the fundamental first, then the interval note (ascending for Above, descending for Below). Compound adds one octave (9/8 sounds as 9/4) and is still answered on the 9/8 node; Both picks at random. With Both, a sounding octave is accepted on either the Unison or Octave node.

Fundamental

A new fundamental is drawn for every interval. "Any" allows any frequency in the range; the tempered options use only 12 or 24 equal steps per octave (A = 440 Hz).

Examples: 440 or A4 +0c (octave 4 is assumed if omitted).

Grid & Pool

Sound

Every note has a 20 ms attack. Sustain holds a steady level; Decay fades over exactly the duration. In successive mode each note lasts the full duration.
